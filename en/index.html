PROJECTS & DIALOGUE

From dialogue, toward practices that engage with society.

aquira.org is the official site for information on Aquira’s dialogue, collaborations, and projects that engage with society. For works and artist information, visit aquira.art; for records of the name and its history, visit aquira1978.com.

Explore practice principles

PURPOSE

Giving dialogue its context.

Opening dialogue

We create an entry point for dialogue around works and questions—one that does not hurry toward conclusions. Even before the terms or purpose have been put into words, we value room to think.

Fostering collaboration

Respecting each other’s backgrounds, time, and roles, we explore possibilities for collaboration where expression and learning meet.

Documenting practice

Information on projects is published as a record in which the people involved, dates, locations, and content can be verified. Unconfirmed plans or outcomes are not presented as established accomplishments.

CONTACT

A quiet beginning for dialogue.

Enquiries are welcome concerning projects, collaboration, learning spaces, and dialogue that takes works as its point of departure.
